1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PROBLEM STATEMENT
================================================================================
Lok Swar ("The Voice of the People") is a next-generation civic grievance
redressal, participatory democracy, and public infrastructure audit platform
engineered specifically for rural and semi-urban India.

THE CORE RURAL PROBLEM:
- Over 280 million rural citizens in India face illiteracy or digital hesitation.
- Standard e-governance systems (CPGRAMS, district portal forms) require typed
  English or formal Hindi text and navigation of multi-layered dropdown trees.
- Rural citizens speak colloquial dialects (Bhojpuri, Odia, Bengali, Magahi,
  Maithili) that standard government portals fail to understand.
- Millions of impoverished citizens possess only basic Rs. 800 feature phones
  (Nokia, JioPhone) with physical keypads and no mobile internet.
- Public infrastructure suffers from "Ghost Works" — contractors billing for
  borewells, road repairs, and school roofs that were never actually executed.

THE LOK SWAR PARADIGM:
1. Zero-Literacy Voice-First UX: Tap anywhere on the screen to hear audio instructions.
2. Dialect Conformance: Automatically recognizes and responds in regional languages.
3. Auto-Synced GPS Telemetry: No manual "Refresh GPS" buttons; automatic satellite
   coordinates and real-time movement tracking.
4. Multi-Sensor Data Fusion: Cross-references citizen claims against satellite
   imagery, IMD rain radar, and drone reconnaissance to verify ground truth.
5. Toll-Free Keypad IVR: Keypad phone users can dial in, select language via DTMF,
   speak their issue, and receive an SMS ticket tracking code.
6. Mathematical Budget Optimization: 0/1 Knapsack Integer Linear Programming
   allocates constrained government funds to projects that maximize human impact.

2. COMPREHENSIVE BREAKDOWN OF EVERY FEATURE
================================================================================

2.1 CITIZEN WEB EXPERIENCE (citizen.html)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Zero-Literacy Every-Click Spoken Guidance:
   - When an illiterate citizen clicks or taps anywhere on the screen (margins,
     cards, titles, or buttons), the Web Audio Speech engine immediately speaks
     conversational orientation in their native regional dialect.
2. Auto-Synchronized GPS Telemetry:
   - On page load, the browser queries satellite GPS in high-accuracy mode.
   - If GPS is unavailable, it gracefully falls back to server reverse-geocoded
     network IP coordinates.
   - Registers navigator.geolocation.watchPosition in the background; movement
     greater than 500 meters automatically re-syncs locality and dialect.
   - Zero manual "Refresh GPS" buttons required.
3. Location-Driven UI Language Adaptation:
   - Geographic coordinates dynamically trigger the regional dialect engine:
     * Bihar / Purvanchal (Patna, Gaya, Bhojpur)       -> Bhojpuri ('bho')
     * Odisha (Bhubaneswar, Sundargarh, Khordha, Puri) -> Odia ('or')
     * West Bengal (Kolkata, Howrah)                   -> Bengali ('bn')
     * North India (Delhi, UP, MP, Rajasthan, Haryana) -> Hindi ('hi')
     * Other / International                           -> English ('en')
   - Automatically adapts all interface elements (headers, cards, badges, and
     spoken prompts) into the citizen's regional language.
4. Continuous Search Bar Auto-Translation:
   - As the citizen speaks or types in their local dialect, a dual-layer Neural
     Machine Translation (NMT) engine translates colloquial phrases into formal
     Administrative English in real time inside the search bar.
5. Multimodal Grievance Ingestion:
   - Audio: In-browser Web Audio recorder capturing live voice notes (WAV/WebM).
   - Camera: Live photo evidence capture of road collapses, flooded classrooms,
     or broken handpumps.
   - GPS: Precise latitude, longitude, and accuracy radius.
   - Text: Optional manual text typing for literate citizens.
6. Democratic Civic Upvoting (Crowdsourced Verification):
   - Citizens can browse active local issues on an interactive Leaflet map and
     click "Endorse / Upvote", demonstrating collective community consensus and
     preventing duplicate ticket spam.
7. DigiLocker & Aadhaar Profile Integration:
   - Citizen identity verification with 12-digit Aadhaar simulation, automatic
     privacy masking (e.g. "•••• •••• 9012"), and irreversible SHA-256 mobile
     hashing to protect citizen anonymity.

2.2 EXECUTIVE & ADMINISTRATIVE DASHBOARD (admin.html)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. GIS Spatial Heatmap & Density Clustering:
   - Interactive Leaflet.js map with CartoDB Positron and Dark Matter tiles
     visualizing infrastructure breakdowns and localized risk densities.
2. Autonomous Drone (UAV) Reconnaissance Center:
   - Dispatches simulated autonomous drone flights to survey high-urgency disaster
     zones (e.g. collapsed bridges, high-voltage power hazards).
   - Displays live UAV flight waypoints, speed, battery telemetry, and geotagged
     4K video feeds.
3. Multi-Sensor Data Fusion Center:
   - Cross-references citizen grievance reports against independent satellite
     imagery, IMD rain radar, and drone footage to calculate an automated
     Fusion Confidence Score (0-100%).
4. Algorithmic Treasury Budget Allocation:
   - Matches verified grievances directly against official state and central
     funding schemes (PMGSY, Jal Jeevan Mission, SDRF, DDUGJY, 5T High School Fund).
5. Contractor Adjudication & Accountability Matrix:
   - Comprehensive audit trail tracking maintenance SLAs, dispute resolutions,
     and breach penalties against failing contractors.

2.3 TOLL-FREE KEYPAD IVR TELEPHONY SYSTEM (ivr/)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Keypad Phone Accessibility:
   - Asynchronous FastAPI server integrated with Twilio Voice API and TwiML.
   - Accessible from any Rs. 800 non-smartphone with zero internet connection.
2. DTMF Dialpad Menu:
   - Press 1 -> Odia
   - Press 2 -> Hindi
   - Press 3 -> Bhojpuri
   - Press 4 -> Bengali
3. Automated Voice Recording & SMS Receipt:
   - The citizen speaks their grievance after the beep. The audio file is
     recorded, transcribed via speech recognition, assigned a unique Ticket ID,
     and an SMS receipt is sent back to their keypad phone.

2.4 GATEWAY PORTAL & CONSTITUENCY TRACKER (index.html)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Public gateway providing transparent tracking of constituency progress,
  panchayat-level performance metrics, and quick navigation between citizen
  and administrative portals.

3. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE, NLP & AUDIO PIPELINES
================================================================================

3.1 SPEECH-TO-TEXT (ASR)
- Engines: Web Speech API (webkitSpeechRecognition) & Google Speech API.
- Supported Languages: Odia (or-IN), Bhojpuri/Hindi (hi-IN), Bengali (bn-IN),
  and English (en-IN).
- Fallback: Server-side Python speech_recognition module with PyAudio.

3.2 COLLOQUIAL DIALECT NORMALIZATION
- Engine: Tokenized Regex & Grammatical Phrase Transducer.
- Normalizes regional spoken idioms into structured concepts:
  * "पुलवा बह गइल बा" -> "The bridge has been washed away"
  * "लाइन नइखे"        -> "There is no electricity"
  * "छतवा चुअता"       -> "The classroom roof is leaking"
  * "चापाकल खराब बा"   -> "The drinking water handpump is broken"

3.3 NEURAL MACHINE TRANSLATION (NMT)
- Tier 1: Google Mobile Translation Engine.
- Tier 2: Google GTX NMT Translation API.
- Tier 3: MyMemory Multi-Lingual API with ISO Language Pair routing.
- Tier 4: Local infrastructure bilingual phrasebook fallback.

3.4 NLP AUTOMATIC CATEGORIZATION
- Categorizes grievances into 1 of 7 Civic Domains:
  1. Power & Electricity (Transformers, fallen poles, wire hazards)
  2. Roads & Connectivity (Collapsed bridges, road washouts, potholes)
  3. Drinking Water & RWSS (Broken handpumps, pipeline leaks, contamination)
  4. Education & 5T Schools (Classroom roof leaks, student safety)
  5. Healthcare & PHC (Doctor absenteeism, ambulance delays, clinic access)
  6. Drainage & Flood Mitigation (Monsoon waterlogging, blocked sewers)
  7. Canal Irrigation & Agriculture (Canal breaches, crop water distress)

3.5 DYNAMIC PRIORITY & URGENCY RISK SCORING
- Formula:
  Urgency = Base_Category_Score + Hazard_Boosts + Verification_Boost
- Base Scores: Healthcare (95.0), Power (94.0), Roads (92.5), Water (89.0)
- Emergency Keywords (+5.5 boost): "collapse", "washout", "flood", "spark", "hazard"
- Verified (+2.5 boost): Peer upvote or DigiLocker-backed citizen.

3.6 GOVERNMENT SCHEME MATCHER
- Automatically pairs identified categories to official government funding:
  * Roads       -> PMGSY (Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana) / SDRF
  * Water       -> Jal Jeevan Mission (RWSS Har Ghar Jal)
  * Electricity -> Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Gram Jyoti Yojana (DDUGJY)
  * Schools     -> 5T High School Transformation Fund / Samagra Shiksha
  * Healthcare  -> National Health Mission (NHM) / BSKY
  * Irrigation  -> Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY)

3.7 TEXT-TO-SPEECH (TTS) & SOUND ENGINE
- Web Speech Synthesis API (speechSynthesis) streaming spoken guidance.
- Custom Web Audio API (AudioContext) synthesizer generating low-latency UI chimes
  (tap chime, success chime, alert tone, recording pulse).

3.8 MULTI-SENSOR DATA FUSION ENGINE
- Correlates citizen reports against:
  1. Satellite Reflectance: Detects surface water accumulation and flood cuts.
  2. IMD Weather Telemetry: Verifies if rainfall actually occurred in that GP.
  3. Autonomous Drone Video: 4K geotagged visual proof of physical assets.
  4. Civic Quorum: Peer upvotes from citizens within 2 km.
- Computes Fusion Confidence Score (0% to 100%) to prevent fraudulent contractor claims.

3.9 0/1 KNAPSACK RESOURCE ALLOCATION OPTIMIZER
- When state funds are constrained, solves the integer linear programming problem:
  Maximize: Z = SUM [ Urgency_i * Population_i * RiskMultiplier_i * X_i ]
  Subject to: SUM [ Cost_i * X_i ] <= Total_Treasury_Budget
  Where: X_i in {0, 1} (Whether project 'i' is funded in current tranche)
- Ensures public funds are allocated scientifically to save the most lives.

6. JUDGE CROSS-QUESTION DEFENSE STRATEGIES (HACKATHON VIVA CHEAT SHEET)
================================================================================

Q1: "How do you prevent duplicate grievances from spamming the system?"
A1: Lok Swar utilizes a Spatial-Semantic Deduplication Filter. When a report is
    submitted, the backend runs a spatial query within a 500-meter radius
    (using Haversine distance) and computes cosine similarity on the category
    and text. If a match is found, instead of creating a duplicate ticket, the
    system automatically converts the submission into a Civic Endorsement
    (+1 Upvote) on the existing active ticket.

Q2: "What if there is no internet in remote rural hamlets?"
A2: Lok Swar operates on a Tri-Tier Connectivity Fallback:
    1. Full Internet: Citizen smartphone portal with interactive map and voice.
    2. Intermittent Internet: LocalStorage offline caching queue that stores
       submissions locally and auto-syncs when connectivity returns.
    3. Zero Internet / Keypad Phones: Our toll-free IVR telephony server allows
       citizens with Rs. 800 feature phones to dial in, select language via
       DTMF keypad, speak their issue, and receive an SMS tracking ticket.

Q3: "How do illiterate citizens navigate the application?"
A3: Through our Every-Click Spoken Orientation Engine. Every button, category
    card, banner, and input has an audio script in js/citizen/voice_engine.js.
    When an illiterate citizen taps anywhere on the screen, the system
    immediately speaks conversational instructions in their native dialect
    (Bhojpuri, Odia, Bengali, Hindi), completely eliminating the need to read
    or type.

Q4: "How does the system ensure mathematical fairness in budget allocation?"
A4: Rather than relying on political discretion, the Lok Swar Knapsack Optimizer
    solves a 0/1 Integer Linear Programming problem:
    Maximize: Z = SUM (Urgency_i * Population_i * RiskMultiplier_i * X_i)
    Subject to: SUM (Cost_i * X_i) <= Total_Treasury_Budget
    This guarantees that public funds are scientifically allocated to projects
    that protect the largest population and avert the highest casualty risks first.

Q5: "Is Aadhaar number storage secure and legal?"
A5: Yes. Lok Swar strictly adheres to the Aadhaar Act guidelines. We never store
    raw 12-digit Aadhaar numbers in plaintext. All identity entries are processed
    through an irreversible SHA-256 cryptographic salt, and only the masked format
    (e.g. '•••• •••• 9012') is displayed on the UI for citizen confirmation.

Q6: "How do you prevent contractors from submitting fake resolution photos?"
A6: Our Multi-Sensor Data Fusion Engine cross-references resolution reports
    against autonomous drone video and IMD weather telemetry. Additionally, a
    grievance is not marked 'Resolved' until a quorum of local citizens who
    endorsed the issue confirm via SMS or web upvote that the work was completed.
