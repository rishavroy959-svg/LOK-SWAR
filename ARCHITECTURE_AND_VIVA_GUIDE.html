SECTION 1

Executive Summary & The 4-Problem Pitch

⚠️ Problem 1: Digital & Literacy Divide

Over 68% of rural citizens in constituencies like Sundargarh cannot type formal complaints in standard English or Hindi. Text-heavy portals like CPGRAMS exclude non-literate and tribal citizens.

⏳ Problem 2: Bureaucratic Triage Delays

Manual staff spend weeks reading, categorizing, and routing complaints. High-urgency emergencies (e.g. collapsed bridge or contaminated water) get buried under routine paperwork.

👻 Problem 3: "Ghost Infrastructure"

Paper government registries claim an asset (e.g. drinking water pump or PMGSY road) is "100% complete", but on the ground the contractor abandoned it or it collapsed unnoticed.

💰 Problem 4: Subjective Political Budgeting

Constituency funds (₹10–50 Cr under DMF or MLA-LAD) are allocated subjectively or politically, leaving remote tribal panchayats perpetually underfunded and neglected.

🚀 The Lok Swar Solution

Lok Swar eliminates literacy barriers with a one-tap voice microphone and basic feature phone IVR, normalizes colloquial rural dialects (Bhojpuri, Odia, Bengali, Hindi), cross-references claims against official registries to expose ghost infrastructure via autonomous survey drones, and mathematically optimizes constituency budget spending using a 0-1 Knapsack MILP solver with mandatory rural equity quotas.

SECTION 3

The 7 Core Subsystems

1. Citizen Voice & Spatial Web Portal citizen.html

Standalone React 18 interface designed for zero-literacy citizens. Features one-tap speech recording, live multi-dialect translation across 5 languages, 50km Haversine proximity radar, and Web Audio API synthesized tactile sound chimes (playChime).

2. District Administrative Command Suite admin.html

Command console for the District Magistrate and Planning Officers. Features an automated 12-factor MAUT triage matrix, Leaflet multi-layer GIS with ESRI satellite photography, MAVLink drone flight simulator, HTML5 Canvas defect computer vision, and a 0-1 Knapsack budget sanction console.

3. Primary REST Server & Audio Ingestion Microservice server.py (Port 8000) & backend/server.js (Port 5000)

Python multi-threaded HTTP server executing 24+ REST APIs and AI pipelines in-process with zero IPC overhead. Coupled with an Express Node.js microservice equipped with Multer for asynchronous non-blocking ingestion of multipart audio recordings (.webm and .wav).

4. Telephony & Low-Tech Feature Phone IVR ivr/main.py (Port 8001)

FastAPI + Motor async MongoDB webhook engine compatible with Twilio, Exotel, and Knowlarity TwiML protocols. Allows non-smartphone owners to dial a toll-free number, navigate menus using phone keypad DTMF tones (1-9), and record audio grievances over standard GSM voice channels.

5. Multi-Dialect NLP & Statutory Scheme Matcher services/translation_ai.py

Intercepts colloquial rural tokens ('बा', 'नइखे', 'पुलवा', 'चापाकल') and executes a 3-tier translation pipeline (Google GTX -> MyMemory -> Civic Domain Lexicon). Automatically classifies grievances into official government schemes (PMGSY, SDRF, Jal Jeevan Mission, 5T Transformation).

6. Constituency Planning & Mathematical Decision Engine services/constituency_engine.py

Implements 12-factor Multi-Attribute Utility Theory (MAUT) scoring and solves the 0-1 Knapsack Mixed-Integer Linear Program (MILP) under a ₹10 Crore budget cap with statutory rural equity constraints (RuralQuota >= 2). Cross-references citizen reports with official registries to catch "Ghost Infrastructure".

7. Dual-Persistence & Zero-Trust Telecom Security db/mongo.py & services/sms_service.py

Active-passive dual storage: primary MongoDB Atlas with transparent, zero-downtime failover to an atomic local JSON cache (db/local_store.json) during village internet cuts. Integrates Fast2SMS for 6-digit OTPs (300s TTL) and enforces UIDAI masked Aadhaar compliance (XXXX-XXXX-1940) to prevent Sybil bot voting.

SECTION 5

Architecture Decision Records: "Why X Instead of Y?"

ADR 1: Why Dual-Server (Python + Node.js) instead of single-stack?

Rationale: Microservice separation of concerns. Python is the gold standard for AI, NLP, SpeechRecognition, and mathematical linear programming (MILP solvers). However, Node.js with Multer is fundamentally superior at asynchronous, non-blocking binary stream ingestion of multipart audio recordings (.webm/.wav). Decoupling audio uploads to Port 5000 ensures heavy binary streams never block REST API queries on Port 8000.

ADR 2: Why React Standalone instead of Next.js / Vite?

Rationale: In rural e-Governance kiosks and block offices, machines often lack Node.js runtimes or have restricted corporate firewalls that block npm install. Standalone React requires zero compilation, loads in under 1.2 seconds from local cache, and can be distributed on an offline USB drive to run in remote panchayats.

ADR 3: Why MongoDB + Atomic JSON instead of PostgreSQL?

Rationale: Civic grievances are polymorphic documents: one report has an array of audio URLs, another has GPS breadcrumbs, another has citizen upvote hashes, and another has drone bounding boxes and MAVLink telemetry. In SQL, this requires 6+ table joins. MongoDB stores these as natural JSON documents. Furthermore, our dual-persistence architecture automatically falls back to an atomic local JSON ledger (db/local_store.json) during village internet blackouts.

ADR 4: Why Leaflet.js instead of Google Maps or Mapbox?

Rationale: Google Maps and Mapbox require proprietary paid API billing keys that easily get rate-limited or disabled during government emergencies. Leaflet.js is completely open-source, ultra-lightweight (42KB vs 500KB+), and allows us to switch dynamically between OpenStreetMap street tiles, CartoDB Dark tactical tiles, and ESRI World Imagery satellite tiles with zero licensing costs.

ADR 5: Why 0-1 Knapsack MILP instead of simple sorting?

Rationale: Greedy sorting fails to maximize overall societal utility under budget constraints, and more dangerously, it introduces geographic bias. If the top 3 projects are expensive urban infrastructure works, greedy sorting exhausts the ₹10 Crore budget, leaving remote tribal villages with ₹0. Our 0-1 Knapsack MILP formulation maximizes total societal value density while enforcing a strict mathematical constraint: Sum(RuralFlag_j * x_j) >= 2, guaranteeing that marginalized rural wards are legally and mathematically protected.

SECTION 6

Top 15 Tough Judge Questions & Winning Answers

Q1: What happens if there is zero internet in a remote panchayat?

Answer: "Lok Swar has a 3-tier offline architecture: (1) Storage fails over to db/local_store.json; (2) NLP falls back to our offline Civic Domain Lexicon; (3) Client audio drafts are cached in IndexedDB until connectivity returns. Citizens without smartphones can also call our IVR line via basic GSM."

Q2: How do you prevent politicians or bots from rigging the upvote system?

Answer: "Every vote requires Fast2SMS OTP verification tied to a verified mobile and masked Aadhaar hash (1-Citizen-1-Vote). Furthermore, project selection is solved mathematically by a 0-1 Knapsack solver, not decided by a discretionary committee."

Q3: What is 'Ghost Infrastructure' and how do you detect it?

Answer: "Ghost infrastructure is when official records mark a project as 100% complete, but on the ground it is washed away or never built. Our Evidence Fusion engine flags discrepancies between citizen complaints and official registries, auto-dispatching survey drones with Computer Vision to capture physical proof."

Q4: How does the system scale from Sundargarh to all of India?

Answer: "Lok Swar is architected hierarchically: Ward -> Gram Panchayat -> Block -> Assembly Constituency -> District -> State. The backend APIs are stateless, database collections are indexed by geohash, and the platform can be containerized on Kubernetes to plug into CPGRAMS."

Q5: Why did you lock language preference on refresh?

Answer: "First-time visitors are automatically initialized in their regional dialect based on GPS reverse geocoding. However, if a user explicitly chooses English or Hindi, refreshing the page must respect user sovereignty. We implemented lok_swar_user_lang_locked so telemetry updates coordinates in the background without overriding user language."
