Lok Swar: Comprehensive Architecture, APIs, AI Models & Software Stack

A deep-dive technical blueprint covering every feature, internal/external API, artificial intelligence pipeline, data fusion engine, and zero-literacy interface built for Lok Swar.

1. Executive Overview & The Problem Solved

Lok Swar ("The Voice of the People") is a decentralized, multimodal civic grievance, participatory democracy, and public infrastructure audit platform tailored for rural and semi-urban India.

🚨 The Rural Barrier
Over 280 million rural citizens in India face illiteracy or digital hesitation. Existing portals (CPGRAMS, district grievance forms) require typed English/Hindi text and complex dropdown trees, excluding ground reality.
💡 The Lok Swar Paradigm
Zero-Literacy Voice-First UX: Tap anywhere to listen. Speak in raw regional dialect (Bhojpuri, Odia, Bengali, Hindi). Automatic satellite GPS sync. Multi-sensor verification against ghost work and corruption.

2. Comprehensive Feature Breakdown

A. Citizen Portal Features (citizen.html)

  • Every-Click Spoken Guidance (Zero-Literacy Engine): Illiterate citizens tapping anywhere on the screen trigger immediate conversational speech orientation in their native tongue without needing to read instructions.
  • Automatic GPS & Telemetry Sync: Completely eliminates manual "Refresh GPS" buttons. Automatically acquires satellite coordinates on load, reverse geocodes locality down to Ward/Gram Panchayat, and maintains live movement telemetry via navigator.geolocation.watchPosition.
  • Location-Driven UI Language Adaptation: Automatically detects geographic state/district from GPS/IP and transforms the entire interface (titles, cards, badges, spoken speech) to the local language: Bihar → Bhojpuri Odisha → Odia West Bengal → Bengali North India → Hindi
  • Continuous Search Bar Auto-Translation: As citizens speak or type in their local dialect, a dual-layer NMT pipeline continuously translates colloquial sentences into polished Administrative English in real time directly inside the search bar.
  • Multimodal Grievance Submission: Supports simultaneous submission of raw voice audio recordings (base64 WAV/WebM), camera-captured photo evidence, GPS coordinates, and Aadhaar identity verification.
  • Democratic Civic Upvoting & Crowdsourced Verification: Citizens living in the same Gram Panchayat can view nearby grievances on an interactive Leaflet map and endorse ("Upvote") them, boosting priority without filing redundant duplicate reports.
  • Profile & DigiLocker Identity Integration: Seamless Aadhaar/Voter ID verification fetching simulated verified credentials while preserving citizen privacy through SHA-256 mobile salting.

B. Executive & Administrative Dashboard (admin.html)

  • Multi-Dimensional Spatial Heatmaps: Real-time GIS rendering with Leaflet.js showcasing high-density infrastructure breakdown clusters using spatial clustering algorithms.
  • Autonomous UAV Drone Surveillance Dispatch: Administrative dispatch of drone recon missions to verify high-urgency road washouts, electrical hazards, or school roof collapses before allocating state budget.
  • Algorithmic Treasury Budget Allocation: Interactive budget ledger matching civic grievances directly against official state and central funding schemes (e.g. Jal Jeevan Mission, PMGSY, SDRF Disaster Relief, 5T Transformation).
  • Multi-Sensor Data Fusion Center: Correlates citizen reports against independent ground sensors, weather satellite feeds, and satellite SAR to detect false or exaggerated claims.
  • Contractor Adjudication & Accountability Matrix: Automated tracking of maintenance SLAs, breach penalties, and public contractor resolution logs.

5. Multi-Sensor Data Fusion Architecture

To prevent corruption and "ghost works" (contractors billing for unperformed repairs), Lok Swar introduces an automated Cross-Verification Matrix:

📡 Satellite Imagery Feed
Compares historical satellite baselines with current surface reflectance to verify road inundation, river overflows, and structural bridge collapses.
🛰️ Autonomous UAV Drone Recon
Dispatches automated drone missions to capture 4K geotagged video and high-resolution still frames, feeding directly into administrative verification tickets.
🌧️ Weather & Rain Telemetry
Cross-references IMD rain telemetry to confirm whether reported flash floods or drainage overflows match real-time precipitation in that Gram Panchayat.
👥 Civic Endorsement Quorum
Requires minimum threshold of peer citizen upvotes from residents living within 2km, creating an immune system against fraudulent single-actor reports.

6. Knapsack Resource Optimizer

When government budgets are constrained (e.g. ₹50,00,000 available vs ₹3,20,00,000 needed), the Lok Swar Optimizer solves a 0/1 Multi-Criteria Knapsack Problem: 

Maximize: Z = ∑ [ Urgency_Score_i × Affected_Population_i × Risk_Multiplier_i × X_i ]
Subject to: ∑ [ Cost_i × X_i ] ≤ Total_Allocated_Treasury_Budget
Where: X_i ∈ {0, 1} (Whether project 'i' is funded in current tranche)

This replaces political bias with mathematically optimal resource allocation, ensuring projects that save the most human lives and protect critical transit arteries are funded first.

7. Keypad Phone IVR Telephony System (ivr/)

Recognizing that millions of citizens own ₹800 basic feature phones (Nokia/JioPhone with physical numeric keypads and no internet), Lok Swar includes an independent toll-free IVR engine:

  • Architecture: FastAPI asynchronous server (ivr.main:app) running on port 8001, integrated with Twilio Voice APIs and TwiML response generators.
  • DTMF Dialpad Menu:
    • Press 1 → ଓଡ଼ିଆ (Odia)
    • Press 2 → हिन्दी (Hindi)
    • Press 3 → भोजपुरी (Bhojpuri)
    • Press 4 → বাংলা (Bengali)
  • Voice Recording & Audio Pipeline: The citizen speaks their grievance after the beep. Twilio records the call audio, posts the URL to /ivr/voice-end, and the AI engine automatically transcribes the audio, generates a ticket ID, and sends an SMS receipt back to their keypad phone.

8. Autonomous Drone Reconnaissance & GIS Geofencing

The admin.html GIS engine utilizes Leaflet.js with customized OpenStreetMap CartoDB dark/light tiles:

  • GeoJSON Constituency Boundaries: Visualizes district administrative sectors (Sundargarh, Hemgir, Lephripara, Tangarpali, Bargaon) with color-coded risk envelopes.
  • Drone Flight Dispatch Simulator: Calculates straight-line distance, battery consumption, estimated arrival time (ETA), and generates automated UAV flight waypoints.

11. Hackathon Cross-Question Defense Strategies

Q1: "How do you prevent duplicate grievances from spamming the system?"

Answer: Lok Swar uses a Spatial-Semantic Deduplication Filter. When a grievance is submitted, the backend runs a spatial query within a 500-meter radius (using Haversine distance) and computes word embedding cosine similarity on the category and text. If a match is found, instead of creating a duplicate ticket, the system automatically converts the submission into a Civic Endorsement (+1 Upvote) on the existing active ticket.

Q2: "What if there is no internet in remote rural hamlets?"

Answer: Lok Swar is built on a Tri-Tier Connectivity Fallback:

  1. Full Internet: Citizen smartphone portal with live map and camera.
  2. Intermittent Internet: LocalStorage offline queue that records grievances locally and auto-synchronizes as soon as 2G/3G connectivity is detected.
  3. Zero Internet / Keypad Phones: Toll-free IVR telephony server (ivr/) where citizens dial in via standard GSM voice network, speak in their native tongue, and receive an SMS tracking ID.

Q3: "How do you handle illiterate citizens who cannot read error messages or submit forms?"

Answer: The Every-Click Spoken Assistant. Every element on the screen has an audio mapping in js/citizen/voice_engine.js. The moment an illiterate citizen taps anywhere, the Web Audio engine speaks clear instructions in their local dialect (Bhojpuri, Odia, Bengali, Hindi), accompanied by high-contrast visual icons and audio chimes.

Q4: "Is Aadhaar number storage secure and legal?"

Answer: Yes. Lok Swar strictly adheres to the Aadhaar Act guidelines. We never store raw 12-digit Aadhaar numbers in plaintext. All identity entries are processed through an irreversible SHA-256 cryptographic salt, and only the masked format (e.g. •••• •••• 9012) is displayed on the UI for citizen confirmation.
