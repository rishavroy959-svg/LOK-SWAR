1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & MISSION STATEMENT
================================================================================
Lok Swar (लोक स्वर) / People's Priorities is an advanced civic intelligence, 
multi-dialect voice grievance intake, GIS spatial analysis, autonomous drone 
verification, and mathematical budget allocation platform.

Core Problems Addressed:
1. The Digital & Literacy Divide: Rural citizens unable to type formal text can 
   submit voice grievances in local dialects (Bhojpuri/Bihari, Odia, Bengali, Hindi).
2. Administrative Delays: Spoken audio is instantly transcribed, converted into 
   Administrative English/Hindi, categorized across 7+ departments, matched with 
   statutory government schemes, and assigned an urgency priority score.
3. Information Discrepancy: Physical ground ground-truth is cross-referenced 
   against official administrative registries, satellite data, and IoT sensors.
4. Opaque Resource Allocation: A transparent 12-factor Multi-Attribute Utility 
   Theory (MAUT) and Mixed-Integer Linear Programming (MILP) 0-1 Knapsack solver 
   allocates constituency budgets (e.g. ₹10 Crore pool) with statutory rural equity 
   guarantees.

- Database Collections:
  * citizens: Registered Mobile, Full Name, Village, Masked Aadhaar, Trust Score, Avatar.
  * officers: Administrative ID, Aadhaar, Password, Designation, Department, Jurisdiction.
  * grievances: Full report lifecycle, audio URLs, photo URLs, translations, votes, opinions, notes.
  * budget_schemes: Total pool amount, sanctioned funds, utilization percentages.
  * drone_missions: Flight telemetry, 3D orthomosaic resolutions, volumetric damage metrics.
  * voice_notes: Standalone voice recording submissions and review statuses.

4.4 12-FACTOR PRIORITY SCORING & 0-1 KNAPSACK MILP OPTIMIZER (js/services/optimizer.js)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Computes priority scores using Multi-Attribute Utility Theory (MAUT):
  * Citizen Demand Intensity (20%)
  * Problem Severity / Threat to Life (15%)
  * Affected Population Size (15%)
  * Infrastructure Gap Distance (15%)
  * Accessibility / Monsoon Cutoff Deficit (10%)
  * Socio-Economic & Tribal Vulnerability (10%)
  * Evidence Confidence Level (10%)
  * Engineering Feasibility & Readiness (5%)
- Optimization Algorithm:
  Solves a Mixed-Integer Linear Programming 0-1 Knapsack problem against the constituency 
  budget cap (e.g. ₹10.0 Crore), ordering candidate projects by Value Density while enforcing 
  a statutory Minimum Rural Equity quota (ensuring remote tribal villages are never deprioritized).

4.5 MULTI-SOURCE EVIDENCE FUSION & DISCREPANCY DETECTION (js/services/evidence_fusion.js)

- Tri-Factor Evidence Formula:
  Composite Score = (Demand Score * 0.35) + (Registry Score * 0.35) + (Verification Multiplier * 100 * 0.30)
- Automated Discrepancy Detection:
  Identifies discrepancies between citizen perceptions and official government registries 
  (e.g., road marked 'operational' in PMGSY registry vs. ground reality of culvert collapse).

4.6 AUTONOMOUS DRONE TELEMETRY & COMPUTER VISION SIMULATOR (js/components/drone_simulator.js)

8. MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & ALGORITHMIC EQUATIONS
================================================================================

1. HAVERSINE DISTANCE FORMULA (50km Civic Proximity Scanner):
   Given point P1(lat1, lng1) and P2(lat2, lng2):
   dLat = radians(lat2 - lat1)
   dLng = radians(lng2 - lng1)
   a = sin²(dLat / 2) + cos(radians(lat1)) * cos(radians(lat2)) * sin²(dLng / 2)
   c = 2 * atan2(sqrt(a), sqrt(1 - a))
   Distance (km) = Earth_Radius (6371.0 km) * c

2. MULTI-ATTRIBUTE UTILITY THEORY (MAUT) 12-FACTOR SCORE:
   Priority_Score = (Demand_Score * 0.20) +
                    (Severity_Score * 0.15) +
                    (Normalized_Population_Score * 0.15) +
                    (Infrastructure_Gap_Score * 0.15) +
                    (Accessibility_Gap_Score * 0.10) +
                    (Socio_Economic_Impact_Score * 0.10) +
                    (Evidence_Confidence_Score * 0.10) +
                    (Feasibility_Score * 0.05)

3. COMPOSITE VALUE DENSITY (For Knapsack Sorting):
   Benefit_Per_Crore = (Priority_Score * (Population / 1000.0)) / max(0.1, Cost_In_Crores)
   Value_Density = (Priority_Score * 0.60) + (Benefit_Per_Crore * 0.40)

4. 0-1 KNAPSACK MIXED-INTEGER LINEAR PROGRAMMING (MILP) FORMULATION:
   Maximize:     Σ (Value_Density_j * x_j)  for all candidate projects j in {1..N}
   Subject to:   Σ (Cost_j * x_j) <= Total_Budget_Cap (e.g. ₹10.0 Cr)
                 Σ (Rural_Flag_j * x_j) >= Min_Rural_Quota (e.g. 2 projects)
                 x_j in {0, 1}
